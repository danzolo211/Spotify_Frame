GraceFrame

A little frame that shows your music, and Scripture over your day.

Hi Emily,

This is a small e-ink picture frame I made for you. When you play music on Spotify, it shows what’s playing — the album art, the song, lyrics when they’re available, and how far along it is. When the music stops, it quietly turns into a Bible verse over a hand-drawn scene, and changes to a new one every so often. It also has a tiny phone app so you can browse verses, save the ones you like, choose the artwork, and see messages that pop up on it.

It only takes a few minutes to set up. Just follow these steps in order.

Step 1  ·  Plug it in

Connect the frame to power with the USB-C cable (any phone charger or a laptop port works). After a few seconds it wakes up and shows a welcome screen.

Step 2  ·  Connect it to your Wi-Fi

The frame will show a “Let’s get connected” screen. On your phone:

  1. Open your phone’s Wi-Fi settings and tap the network named GraceFrame-Setup.
  2. A setup page opens by itself (if it doesn’t, open your browser and it’ll appear). Pick your home Wi-Fi from the list — or type its name if you don’t see it — and enter your Wi-Fi password. (Choose the 2.4 GHz network if yours lists two. You’re typing your password into the frame, not sending it to anyone.)
  3. Tap Save & Connect. The frame restarts, joins your Wi-Fi, then shows a connected screen with its address. If the “GraceFrame-Setup” screen comes back, the password was probably mistyped — just do it once more.

Step 3  ·  Put the app on your phone

First make sure your phone is back on your home Wi-Fi (the same one you just gave the frame). Then open the app one of two ways:

  1. Holding a paper copy? Point your phone’s camera at the square code below, and tap the link that pops up.
  2. Reading this on your phone? Just tap graceframe.local (or type graceframe.local into your browser).
Opens your GraceFrame app  ·  graceframe.local

When the app opens, tap your browser’s Share button and choose Add to Home Screen. Now it lives on your phone like a real app, one tap away. (If “graceframe.local” won’t open the first time, wait a minute and try again — the frame just needs a moment to announce itself.)

Your Music (Spotify)

Your Spotify is already connected — you don’t set anything up. To see your music on the frame:

  1. Open Spotify on your phone (or laptop, or any device you’re signed into with your account).
  2. Play any song.
  3. Glance at the frame — within a few seconds it shows the album art, the song name, a progress bar, and synced lyrics for songs that have them.

That’s the whole thing. When you pause or stop, the frame drifts back to Scripture after a few minutes. Nothing showing after ~10 seconds? Check that the song is actually playing (not paused) and that it’s on your own Spotify account.

What the app can do

  1. Now — a live mirror of exactly what the frame is showing.
  2. Verses — search or browse every verse; tap the heart to save a favorite (saved ones show up more often), or send one to the frame.
  3. Scenes — pick which hand-drawn artwork sits behind the words.
  4. Notes — every so often a little handwritten note will appear on the frame (a hello from me). It stays a while, then the verses return. You can also wave one away here.
  5. Settings — how often verses change, quiet hours (it rests at night and holds one calm verse), and more.

Good to know: it’s okay to leave it plugged in all the time — it sips almost no power and looks after its own screen. If the power ever blips or your router restarts, it sorts itself out and comes right back on its own. Nothing you can do will break it.
