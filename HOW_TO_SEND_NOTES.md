# How you send Emily notes

A note appears on her frame in handwriting, stays a while, then the verses
return. You can send one two ways.

## The easy way — from anywhere (recommended)

Works on any Wi-Fi or on cell data. You do **not** have to be at her place.

1. **One-time setup:** open **`graceframe_remote_qr.png`** (in this folder) on your
   computer screen and **scan it with your phone's camera**. Tap the link that pops
   up — it opens your private *"Send a note"* page.
2. On that page, tap your browser's **Share → Add to Home Screen** so it's one tap
   away from now on.
3. **To send a note:** open it, type your message, add your name, choose how long it
   should stay on the frame, and tap **Send to frame**. It shows up on her frame
   within a few seconds.

That link is your personal sender — keep it to yourself.

## The other way — when you're on her Wi-Fi

Open the app (**graceframe.local**) → **Notes** tab → type your message → **Send to
frame**. Same result, but this one only works while you're on her home Wi-Fi.

## Good to know

- Notes travel through a free public relay (ntfy.sh), so keep them sweet, not secret.
- If a note won't appear: make sure the frame is powered and on Wi-Fi, and that you
  used the **current** sender link (the one from `graceframe_remote_qr.png`).
- You can dismiss a note early from the app's **Notes** tab (**Dismiss current**).
